
## Timing

Archive within 24 hours of Finance Lead approval.

## Steps
1. **Verify completeness:**
        - [ ] Source_Data/ has all exports
    - [ ] Statements/ has all bank/card statements
    - [ ] Workpapers/ has checklist, budget, recon docs
2. **Move to archive:**
        - From: `/Finance/Monthly Reconciliation/2024-11/`
    - To: `/Finance/Archive/Monthly Reconciliation/2024-11/`
3. **Set permissions:**
        - Right-click → Share → General Access
    - Set to "Viewer" only (remove Editor)
4. **Verify backup:**
        - Confirm Google Drive sync active
5. **Log in Quaestor:**
        - Status → "Complete"
    - Record completion date
